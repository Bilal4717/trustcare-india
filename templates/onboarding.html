Step 01 / 04

Ask complex, multi-attribute care questions

Unlike a simple search box, TrustCare combines location, procedure, equipment, specialties, and staffing signals into a single reasoning query — and returns ranked matches with citations.

Try asking:
"Find the nearest facility in rural Bihar that can perform an emergency appendectomy and typically leverages part-time doctors."

"Which hospitals in Jharkhand have a working ICU with ventilator support?"
📍

Location-Aware

Filters by state, city, PIN code, and nearest-to proximity using Haversine distance.

🔬

Procedure Matching

Searches equipment logs, unstructured descriptions, and specialty tags simultaneously.

🧠

LLM Reasoning

Gemini 2.5 Flash / Meta-Llama 3 synthesises all signals into a ranked, cited answer.

🌐

Web Enrichment

Tavily live web search optionally enriches answers with current facility information.

Step 02 / 04

Verify capability claims before trusting them

A facility listing "Advanced Surgery" is not the same as one that actually has an Anesthesiologist and a functional Operation Theatre. The Audit agent cross-checks every claim against evidence from the facility's own records.

What the Audit tab does:
1. Extracts structured capabilities from free-form facility text
2. Cross-references claimed capabilities against reported equipment
3. Marks each as ✓ Verified, ⚑ Missing evidence, or ? Uncertain
4. Returns a verification ratio and row-level evidence snippet
✓

Verified

Capability is both claimed AND supported by evidence in the facility record.

⚑

Missing Evidence

Claimed but not backed by equipment, staff, or procedure evidence — flag for investigation.

📄

Row-Level Citations

Every verdict cites the exact phrase from the facility record that supports or contradicts it.

📊

Verification Ratio

A single score (0–100%) summarising how much of the facility's claims are evidence-backed.

Step 03 / 04

Understand the Trust Score and confidence intervals

Every recommendation comes with a trust score, contradiction flags, and a bootstrap-based 95% confidence interval — so you know not just what the answer is, but how certain to be about it given the data quality.

Confidence Interval example:
Score: 0.72  ·  95% CI: [0.61, 0.83]  ·  Width: 0.22
Wider intervals mean the underlying data is sparse or incomplete. Narrower intervals mean stronger evidence exists for the conclusion.
📐

Bootstrap CI

Non-parametric 500-sample bootstrap estimates uncertainty from actual data distribution.

Step 04 / 04

Use the crisis map to plan interventions

The Desert Finder scans all 10,000+ records grouped by PIN code, city, and state. Any region missing high-acuity specialties is flagged with a priority level and displayed on an interactive India map — ready for NGO and policy planning.

8 tracked high-acuity specialties:
Oncology · Dialysis · Emergency Medicine · Cardiology · Neonatal Medicine · Trauma Surgery · Intensive Care · Neurology

Priority levels: Critical (≥75% missing) · High (≥50%) · Medium (≥25%) · Low
