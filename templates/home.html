Process

From messy records to life-saving insight

01

Intelligent Document Parsing

Databricks processes 10,000+ free-form facility notes — equipment logs, 24/7 claims, specialty listings — and embeds them into Mosaic AI Vector Search.

02

Multi-Agent Reasoning

A LangGraph pipeline runs 8 specialised agents: intent detection, retrieval, auditing, trust scoring, desert finding, validation, and response building.

03

Verified, Cited Answers

Every answer includes row-level evidence, a trust score, 95% confidence intervals, and a full MLflow 3 trace — so planners can act with confidence.

Trust Scorer

Not all claims are equal

TrustCare evaluates every facility claim against evidence in its own records. An "Advanced Surgery" listing without an Anesthesiologist or functioning OT gets flagged before it reaches a planner.

Scores combine rule-based TRUST_RULES (65%) with LLM assessment (35%) and are always paired with a bootstrap 95% confidence interval.
