How It Works

From messy records to life-saving insight

Intelligent Document Parsing

Databricks processes 10,000+ free-form facility notes — equipment logs, 24/7 claims, specialty listings — and embeds them into a Mosaic AI Vector Search index.

Multi-Agent Reasoning

A LangGraph pipeline orchestrates 8 specialized agents: intent detection, retrieval, auditing, trust scoring, desert finding, validation, and response building.

Verified, Cited Answers

Every answer includes row-level evidence snippets, a trust score, confidence intervals, and a full reasoning trace — so planners and families can trust the output.
